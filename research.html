Plant diversity, environmental variation, and crop research

I study plant diversity, genetic resources, phenotypic traits, and climatic and environmental variation in cultivated plants. My research experience spans botanical collections, plant phenotyping, molecular approaches, phylogenetic research on invasive plants, environmental datasets, and horticultural crop research.

Missouri Botanical Garden

Climatic niche characterization of Mangifera indica

This project examined climatic variation associated with cultivated mango using georeferenced botanical records and temperature- and precipitation-related climatic datasets across its regions of domestication and introduction.

Read more about my thesis
2019–2022
South Dakota State University

Disentangling the Complex Evolutionary History of Invasive Russian Thistle

This project investigated the evolutionary history of the Russian thistle group and reconstructed phylogenetic relationships using target-capture sequencing and bioinformatics approaches, while supporting extension publications related to group classification, identification, and associated challenges.
