Graduate Research

Research projects

Climatic Patterns of Cultivated Mango

Mangifera indica L.
Saint Louis University & Missouri Botanical Garden · 2022–2026

My graduate research examined climatic conditions associated with cultivated mango records across its region of domestication and introduced regions.

The work used georeferenced botanical records together with environmental data to characterize climatic conditions associated with cultivated mango.

Russian Thistle Diversity & Evolution

Salsola spp.
South Dakota State University · 2019–2022

My graduate research examined phylogenetic relationships, classification, and identification within the Russian thistle group.

The research used molecular and phylogenetic approaches to investigate relationships and hybridization within the group.
