Disentangling the Complex Evolutionary History of Invasive Russian Thistle

This project investigated the evolutionary history of the Russian thistle group and reconstructed phylogenetic relationships using target-capture sequencing and bioinformatics approaches, while supporting extension publications related to group classification, identification, and associated challenges.
