Plant diversity, environmental variation, and crop research

I study plant diversity, genetic resources, phenotypic traits, and climatic and environmental variation in cultivated plants. My research experience spans botanical collections, plant phenotyping, molecular approaches, phylogenetic research, environmental datasets, and horticultural crop research.

COMPLETED RESEARCH PROJECTS (GRADUATE THESIS)

Saint Louis University & Missouri Botanical Garden (2022–2026)

Climatic niche characterization of Mangifera indica

This project examined climatic variation associated with cultivated Mango, using georeferenced botanical records and temperature- and precipitation-related climatic datasets, across its regions of domestication and introduction.

Read more about my thesis
South Dakota State University (2019–2022)

Disentangling the Complex Evolutionary History of Invasive Russian Thistle: Phylogenetic Relationships, the Role of Hybridization, and Public Engagement

This project investigated the evolutionary history of the Russian thistle group and reconstructed phylogenetic relationships using target-capture sequencing and bioinformatics approaches, while supporting extension publications related to group classification, identification, and management challenges.
