Research Projects

Graduate research

Climatic Patterns of Cultivated Mango

Mangifera indica L.

My graduate research at Saint Louis University and the Missouri Botanical Garden examined climatic conditions associated with cultivated mango records across its region of domestication and introduced regions.

The work used georeferenced botanical records together with environmental data to characterize climatic conditions associated with cultivated mango.

Russian Thistle Diversity & Evolution

Salsola spp.

At South Dakota State University, my graduate research examined phylogenetic relationships, classification, and identification within the Russian thistle group.

The research included molecular and phylogenetic approaches to investigate relationships and hybridization within the group.
