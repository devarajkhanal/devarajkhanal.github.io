Field Notes

Observations from research and fieldwork

Fieldwork has been an important part of my experience in horticulture and plant biology. This section will document selected observations, plant collections, field activities, and experiences from research environments.

Horticulture · Perennial Crops

Working with perennial crop systems

Field experience with perennial crops has included planting, plant care, weeding, harvesting, and routine field maintenance. These activities provided practical experience with crop growth, seasonal changes, and hands-on horticultural production.

Vineyard · Fieldwork

Vineyard fieldwork

Vineyard activities included weeding, tendril support, pruning, and general plant care. These experiences provided practical exposure to vineyard maintenance and perennial crop management.

Botanical Collections · Research

Botanical collections and herbarium work

My research experience has included handling plant specimens, herbarium materials, botanical collections, specimen labeling, filing, metadata, documentation, and destructive sampling. These collections provide important resources for plant research and the study of plant diversity.

Plant Phenotyping · Research

Observing plant traits

Plant phenotyping has included plant trait characterization, hyperspectral leaf scanning, and collection of trait-related observations. These approaches help connect observable plant characteristics with environmental variation and plant diversity.
