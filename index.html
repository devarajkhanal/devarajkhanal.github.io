About Me

Connecting plants, environments, and diversity.

Plant Phenotyping & Horticultural Research

Field · Laboratory · Botanical Collections

My research experience includes plant trait characterization, hyperspectral leaf scanning, herbarium and botanical collections, and field-based work with perennial and horticultural crops.

Climatic Adaptation of Cultivated Mango

Mangifera indica L.

My graduate research at Saint Louis University and the Missouri Botanical Garden examined climatic patterns of cultivated mango by comparing environmental conditions across its region of domestication and introduced regions.

My work also draws on extensive literature concerning mango cultivation, domestication, introduction history, geographic origins, and diversity in South and Southeast Asia.

Plant Diversity, Genetics & Evolution

Salsola · Plant Genetic Resources

At South Dakota State University, my research focused on phylogenetic relationships, classification, and identification within the Russian thistle group (Salsola spp.).

I also contributed to molecular research using plant specimens, voucher records, DNA-related laboratory work, and target-capture sequencing approaches.

My broader experience includes Russian thistle identification and management, as well as contribution to DNA laboratory work for an ongoing mango phylogeny project.
