Research Experience

Academic and research experience

Graduate Research Assistant

Saint Louis University · Biology Department · Missouri Botanical Garden / William L. Brown Center
2022–2026

My graduate research focused on the climatic environment of cultivated mango (Mangifera indica L.) and the relationship between cultivated plants and environmental variation.

  • Investigated climatic patterns associated with cultivated mango using georeferenced botanical records and environmental datasets.
  • Examined temperature, precipitation, geographic variation, and climatic conditions associated with cultivated mango.
  • Worked with herbarium specimens, botanical collections, specimen labeling, filing, metadata, and destructive sampling.
  • Contributed to Mangifera phylogenomics through specimen organization and DNA extraction and quality assessment.
  • Conducted hyperspectral leaf scanning and plant trait data collection.
  • Supported perennial crop research and horticultural field activities.

Graduate Research & Teaching Assistant

South Dakota State University
2019–2022

My graduate research at South Dakota State University focused on the evolutionary history, phylogenetics, hybridization, and identification of the Russian thistle group (Salsola spp.).

  • Investigated phylogenetic relationships within the Russian thistle group.
  • Studied plant diversity, classification, identification, and evolutionary relationships.
  • Worked with plant samples, voucher specimens, and molecular records.
  • Participated in target-capture sequencing and related molecular research approaches.
  • Maintained plant sample inventories and associated research records.
  • Contributed to research and teaching activities within the university biology program.
