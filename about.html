About Me

From horticulture to plant research

I am a plant scientist with a background in horticulture and plant biology. My academic training began with horticulture, which developed my interest in plants, crop production, plant diversity, and the relationship between plants and their growing environments.

My graduate research has expanded that foundation into plant diversity, evolutionary relationships, plant genetic resources, environmental adaptation, botanical collections, molecular approaches, and plant phenotyping.

Across these experiences, I have worked with both cultivated and wild plants and have gained experience connecting field observations, botanical collections, environmental information, plant traits, and molecular data.

I am particularly interested in research that connects genetic and phenotypic diversity with environmental variation, especially in horticultural, specialty, and perennial crops.

Research Journey

2017

Horticulture

I completed my B.S. in Horticulture at Tribhuvan University in Nepal, developing a foundation in plant science, crop production, horticultural systems, and plant management.

2019–2022

Plant Diversity & Evolution

At South Dakota State University, my graduate research focused on the Russian thistle group (Salsola spp.), including phylogenetic relationships, hybridization, classification, species identification, and molecular research.

2022–2026

Plant Biology & Environmental Adaptation

At Saint Louis University and the Missouri Botanical Garden, my research focused on cultivated mango and its climatic environment. I examined geographic and environmental patterns associated with cultivated mango while working with botanical collections and environmental datasets.
